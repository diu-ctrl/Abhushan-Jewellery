Crafted in our Ahmedabad studio, the Threadbare Ring represents the beauty of minimalism. Care Guide

Store your solid gold jewellery in the provided soft pouch or a lined jewellery box. Avoid exposure to harsh chemicals, perfumes, chlorine, and hot tubs to prevent scratching and dullness.

Gently clean with warm water, mild dish soap, and a soft-bristled brush. Pat dry with a clean, lint-free cloth. For professional cleaning, visit our Ahmedabad studio.

We offer lifetime polishing and inspection services at our Ahmedabad retail studio. Bring your piece in anytime to restore its original luster.
